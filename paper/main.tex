\begin{abstract}
For independent lattice-valued observations with finite positive variance, conditioning the sum in a bounded central-limit window preserves the complete indexed record of any event whose probability tends to zero, in total variation. We prove a triangular-array version and a joint extension with an additional $o(n)$-coordinate sample under finitely many lattice constraints. Consequences include approximation of every $o(n)$ upper order statistics, Poisson approximation of all rare-value counts even when their total mean diverges, and ranked outdegree comparisons for Galton--Watson trees conditioned on size and a central leaf count. The allocation results answer Janson's fixed-rank question, including its moving-tilt and weak-centering formulations. Counterexamples show that a positive fraction of ranks can retain the sum constraint and that the fixed-tilt logarithmic centering scale cannot generally be enlarged.
\end{abstract}
